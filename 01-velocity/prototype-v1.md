## The build

- **What I built:** My prototype gives users an overview of the state of churn. Primarily highlighting the % churning, the ARR at risk and the main reasons identified for churning. The idea is to highlight areas of improvements to protect more ARR and identifying the main accounts at risk
- **Tool used:** Lovable
- **Shareable link:** https://first-ninety-signal.lovable.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** I understood it right away. It's for new accounts in their first 90 days. It shows which ones are about to leave, why, and how much money is on the line. 30% churn, 9 at risk out of 120, $234k at risk. That part was clear.
- **What confused them:** A few things confused me. It says sample data and live accounts at the same time, so I didn't know which it was. I didn't know how the health score was worked out. The same names kept showing up with different scores, so I wasn't sure if they were the same account or not. The interventions page starts at zero everywhere, so at first it looked like it did nothing. And I couldn't see what to actually do about one account, like Atlas Cloud. The list and the simulator felt separate.
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** I think it's testing that you can spot churn early from a few signs, mainly no onboarding call and the main feature not used by day 14. And that if you show the team who is at risk, why, and what it's worth, they will act in time to save them.
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** My assignment partner was hoping to see a clear idea of why accounts churn, but sometimes this was confusing because the same account was repeated twice. Centralising reasons into a single account and ordering the causes of churn by impact would be better. I think my mistake here was to try and create a different dashboard based on user, meaning every user will have different call to actions to prevent churn.
